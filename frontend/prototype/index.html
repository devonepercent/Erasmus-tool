Erasmus Mundus, in plain words

A fully funded master's across two or more European universities. One application, one scholarship, one degree recognised across Europe.

Who can apply

Any bachelor's graduate, from any country, in any field. No upper age limit. Most programmes ask for an English score and a strong motivation letter.

Common questions

Do I need IELTS?

Most programmes accept IELTS, TOEFL or a medium of instruction certificate. Some waive it entirely.

Is a low CGPA a dealbreaker?

No. Programmes weigh motivation, fit and experience heavily. Many of our admits had under 7.5 CGPA.

How many programmes can I apply to?

Up to three Erasmus Mundus programmes per intake.
